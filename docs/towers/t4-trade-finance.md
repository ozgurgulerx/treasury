# T4: Trade Finance & Collateral

## Overview

Trade finance in oil & gas involves complex instruments (LCs, SBLCs, guarantees) and extensive documentation requirements. AI streamlines document processing, compliance checking, and collateral management.

## Key Use Cases

### LC/SBLC/Guarantee Management
- **Automated instrument lifecycle tracking** from issuance to expiry
- **Renewal and expiry alerts** with AI-driven prioritization
- **Fee optimization** analyzing bank charges across providers
- **Facility utilization monitoring** with limit breach alerts

### SWIFT/MT7xx Message Parsing
- **Automated parsing of MT700, MT760, MT799** and related messages
- **Data extraction and validation** using NLP models
- **Exception identification** for manual review
- **Integration with TMS/ERP** systems

### Document Checking vs. UCP/ISBP
- **AI-powered document examination** against UCP 600 / ISBP rules
- **Discrepancy detection** before presentation to banks
- **Compliance scoring** for trade documents
- **Automated checklist generation** for document review

### Discrepancy Management
- **Discrepancy classification** and severity scoring
- **Resolution recommendation engine** suggesting corrective actions
- **Bank negotiation support** with historical success data
- **Discrepancy trend analysis** for process improvement

### Collateral/Margin Call Forecasting
- **Collateral requirement prediction** based on market scenarios
- **Margin call early warning** using real-time price feeds
- **Collateral optimization** across counterparties
- **Liquidity impact modeling** of margin requirements

## AI/ML Techniques Applied

| Technique | Application |
|-----------|-------------|
| NLP (Document Understanding) | SWIFT message and trade doc parsing |
| Rule-Based AI + ML Hybrid | UCP/ISBP compliance checking |
| Classification Models | Discrepancy categorization |
| Time Series + Monte Carlo | Margin call forecasting |
| Optimization | Collateral allocation |

## Expected Benefits

- **80% faster document processing** through automation
- **Reduced discrepancy rates** via pre-check validation
- **Proactive collateral management** avoiding margin surprises
- **Lower trade finance costs** through fee optimization

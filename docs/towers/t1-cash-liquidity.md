# T1: Cash & Liquidity (ALM)

## Overview

Cash and Liquidity Management is the cornerstone of treasury operations. AI-powered solutions enable real-time visibility, accurate forecasting, and optimal liquidity allocation across the enterprise.

## Key Use Cases

### Cash Positioning
- **Real-time global cash visibility** across all bank accounts and entities
- **Automated cash position aggregation** from multiple ERP and banking systems
- **Intraday liquidity monitoring** with AI-driven anomaly detection

### Multi-Horizon Cash Forecasting
- **Short-term forecasting** (daily/weekly) using ML models trained on historical patterns
- **Medium-term forecasting** (monthly/quarterly) incorporating business drivers
- **Long-term projections** aligned with strategic planning cycles
- **AI-driven variance analysis** identifying forecast vs. actual deviations

### Pooling & Sweeping
- **Intelligent sweep optimization** to minimize idle cash and maximize returns
- **Multi-currency notional pooling** with automated interest optimization
- **Cross-border cash concentration** with regulatory compliance checks

### Liquidity Buffers
- **Dynamic buffer calculation** based on operational requirements and risk appetite
- **Stress-tested liquidity reserves** using multiple scenario models
- **Automated rebalancing** recommendations across liquidity tiers

### Stress/Scenario Analysis for Liquidity
- **Monte Carlo simulations** for liquidity stress testing
- **Scenario modeling** (recession, supply chain disruption, commodity price shocks)
- **Regulatory compliance** with liquidity coverage ratio (LCR) requirements
- **Early warning indicators** for potential liquidity crunches

## AI/ML Techniques Applied

| Technique | Application |
|-----------|-------------|
| Time Series Forecasting (LSTM, Prophet) | Multi-horizon cash flow prediction |
| Anomaly Detection | Unusual cash movements identification |
| Optimization Algorithms | Pooling/sweeping strategy |
| Reinforcement Learning | Dynamic buffer management |
| Ensemble Methods | Scenario probability weighting |

## Expected Benefits

- **50%+ improvement** in forecast accuracy
- **Reduced idle cash** through optimized pooling
- **Proactive liquidity management** vs. reactive crisis handling
- **Regulatory compliance automation** for LCR/NSFR

# T7: Strategic Foresight (Scenario + Constraints)

## Overview

Strategic treasury management requires forward-looking analysis of covenants, market scenarios, and regulatory changes. AI enables proactive scenario planning and constraint management.

## Key Use Cases

### Covenants Early-Warning
- **Covenant headroom monitoring** across all debt agreements
- **Predictive breach detection** based on forecast trajectories
- **Scenario-based covenant stress testing**
- **Remediation recommendations** when breaches approach
- **Covenant compliance dashboards** for CFO/Treasurer

### Macro/Market Scenario Engine
- **Automated scenario generation** based on economic indicators
- **Oil price scenario modeling** (bull/bear/base cases)
- **Interest rate and FX scenario analysis**
- **Geopolitical risk scenario incorporation**
- **Probability-weighted impact assessment**

### Regulatory Shifts Impact
- **Regulatory change monitoring** using NLP on regulatory feeds
- **Impact assessment automation** for new regulations
- **Compliance gap analysis** with remediation planning
- **Tax law change impact** on treasury structures
- **ESG regulation tracking** and reporting requirements

### Treasury Digital Twin Narratives
- **Virtual treasury modeling** simulating operations
- **What-if analysis** for strategic decisions
- **Scenario narratives generation** for board presentations
- **Interactive scenario exploration** dashboards
- **Real-time digital twin updates** with live data

## AI/ML Techniques Applied

| Technique | Application |
|-----------|-------------|
| Monte Carlo Simulation | Scenario generation and stress testing |
| NLP (Transformer Models) | Regulatory monitoring and analysis |
| Predictive Analytics | Covenant breach prediction |
| Generative AI | Narrative and report generation |
| Digital Twin Modeling | Virtual treasury simulation |

## Expected Benefits

- **Early covenant breach warning** (30-90 days advance notice)
- **Proactive regulatory compliance** vs. reactive scrambling
- **Better board communication** through AI-generated narratives
- **Strategic decision support** with digital twin simulations

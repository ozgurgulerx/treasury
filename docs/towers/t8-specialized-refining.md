# T8: Specialized (Refining)

## Overview

Downstream oil & gas operations have unique treasury requirements around vessel operations, fuel procurement, environmental compliance, and insurance. AI addresses these specialized needs.

## Key Use Cases

### Demurrage/Laytime
- **Automated laytime calculation** from vessel documentation
- **Demurrage dispute prediction** and evidence gathering
- **Optimal vessel scheduling** recommendations
- **Demurrage accrual forecasting** for financial planning
- **Port performance analytics** for negotiation support

### Bunker Fuel Management
- **Bunker price forecasting** using ML models
- **Optimal procurement timing** recommendations
- **Supplier performance scoring** and selection
- **Fuel quality monitoring** with anomaly detection
- **Hedging strategy** for bunker fuel exposure

### Crack Spreads
- **Crack spread forecasting** (3-2-1, 5-3-2 spreads)
- **Margin optimization** recommendations
- **Refinery yield optimization** impact on treasury
- **Spread trading strategy** support
- **Regional spread analysis** across markets

### Carbon Credits
- **Carbon credit requirement forecasting**
- **Credit price prediction** for budget planning
- **Compliance pathway optimization** (buy vs. reduce)
- **Carbon credit portfolio management**
- **Regulatory scenario analysis** for carbon pricing

### Insurance/Claims
- **Claims processing automation** using document AI
- **Coverage gap identification** in insurance portfolios
- **Premium optimization** analysis across providers
- **Risk quantification** for insurance decisions
- **Claims outcome prediction** for reserving

### Logistics-Finance Touchpoints
- **Working capital impact** of logistics decisions
- **LC/guarantee requirements** for shipping contracts
- **Freight payment optimization** and timing
- **Inventory financing** linked to in-transit cargo
- **Logistics cost analytics** for treasury planning

## AI/ML Techniques Applied

| Technique | Application |
|-----------|-------------|
| Time Series Forecasting | Price and spread prediction |
| Document AI (OCR + NLP) | Laytime and claims processing |
| Optimization Algorithms | Scheduling and procurement |
| Regression Models | Demurrage and cost prediction |
| Scenario Analysis | Carbon and regulatory modeling |

## Expected Benefits

- **Reduced demurrage costs** through better scheduling
- **Optimized bunker procurement** timing and pricing
- **Proactive carbon compliance** with cost optimization
- **Faster claims processing** and improved recovery

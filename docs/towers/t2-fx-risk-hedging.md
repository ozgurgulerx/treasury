# T2: FX / Rates / Commodity Risk & Hedging

## Overview

Oil & gas companies face significant exposure to foreign exchange rates, interest rates, and commodity prices. AI-powered risk management enables proactive hedging strategies and real-time exposure monitoring.

## Key Use Cases

### Exposure Aggregation
- **Automated exposure capture** from ERP, trading, and treasury systems
- **Real-time FX/commodity exposure dashboards** across all entities
- **Natural hedge identification** across business units and currencies
- **Net exposure calculation** with multi-currency consolidation

### Hedge Strategy & Optimization
- **AI-driven hedge ratio optimization** balancing risk reduction vs. cost
- **Dynamic hedging recommendations** based on market conditions
- **Instrument selection optimization** (forwards, options, swaps, collars)
- **Cost-benefit analysis** of hedging alternatives

### Hedge Effectiveness Testing
- **Automated hedge effectiveness testing** for accounting compliance (IFRS 9, ASC 815)
- **Prospective and retrospective testing** using AI models
- **Hedge documentation automation** with audit trail
- **De-designation alerts** when hedges become ineffective

### Regime Detection
- **Market regime classification** (trending, mean-reverting, volatile)
- **Early warning signals** for regime changes
- **Adaptive hedging strategies** based on detected regimes
- **Correlation breakdown detection** in multi-asset portfolios

### Integrated FX-Commodity Views
- **Cross-asset correlation analysis** (crude vs. USD, product spreads)
- **Integrated risk dashboards** combining FX and commodity exposures
- **Scenario analysis** for combined FX/commodity shocks
- **Portfolio-level VaR** incorporating all market risks

## AI/ML Techniques Applied

| Technique | Application |
|-----------|-------------|
| Hidden Markov Models | Regime detection and classification |
| Deep Learning (Transformers) | Price forecasting and volatility prediction |
| Convex Optimization | Hedge ratio optimization |
| Reinforcement Learning | Dynamic hedging strategy |
| Copula Models | Correlation and dependency modeling |

## Expected Benefits

- **96% forecast accuracy** for FX rate movements
- **20-30% reduction** in hedging costs through optimization
- **Automated compliance** with hedge accounting standards
- **Real-time risk visibility** across all exposures

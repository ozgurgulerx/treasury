# T3: Working Capital

## Overview

Working capital optimization in oil & gas requires balancing massive receivables, payables, and inventory cycles. AI enables predictive analytics and intelligent automation to release trapped cash.

## Key Use Cases

### AR/AP Timing Prediction
- **Payment behavior modeling** predicting when customers will pay
- **Supplier payment optimization** balancing early payment discounts vs. cash retention
- **DSO/DPO forecasting** with AI-driven trend analysis
- **Cash conversion cycle optimization** recommendations

### Collections Optimization
- **Intelligent prioritization** of collection efforts based on ML scoring
- **Customer risk segmentation** for tailored collection strategies
- **Dunning optimization** with AI-driven timing and messaging
- **Dispute prediction** identifying invoices likely to be contested

### Cash Application
- **Automated remittance matching** using NLP and ML
- **Exception handling automation** for complex matches
- **Self-learning algorithms** improving match rates over time
- **Bank statement parsing** with intelligent extraction

### Supply Chain Finance (SCF) Levers
- **Dynamic discounting optimization** identifying optimal discount rates
- **Reverse factoring recommendations** based on supplier analysis
- **SCF program impact modeling** on working capital metrics
- **Supplier financing eligibility scoring** using AI

### Inventory Financing Signals
- **Inventory-to-cash cycle prediction** for financing decisions
- **Collateral valuation models** for inventory-backed financing
- **Demand forecasting** for optimal inventory levels
- **Working capital impact analysis** of inventory decisions

## AI/ML Techniques Applied

| Technique | Application |
|-----------|-------------|
| Survival Analysis | Payment timing prediction |
| NLP (BERT, GPT) | Remittance matching and extraction |
| Gradient Boosting (XGBoost) | Customer payment risk scoring |
| Optimization Algorithms | SCF and discount optimization |
| Demand Forecasting | Inventory financing signals |

## Expected Benefits

- **10-20% cash release** through working capital optimization
- **90%+ auto-match rate** for cash application
- **Reduced DSO** through intelligent collections
- **Optimized supplier financing** with data-driven decisions
